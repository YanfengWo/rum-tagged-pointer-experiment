materialize a 32-byte AtomRef argument and call scan::atom for each visited atom.

Original scan also copies Symbol and calls identity: that accessor alone is
not unique to the new representations. Original small integers calculate directly
in the traversal loop instead of going through the extra AtomRef call boundary.

Interpretation: the common non-inlined projection consumer is a concrete
benchmark/code-generation confounder. Inline atom and atom_ref consistently,
preserve the initial runs, validate semantics, then measure the control again.

reconstructing Symbol; original Symbol already caches id. This structural cost
may remain after inlining, especially for many distinct symbols.
These instructions do not establish how much of any elapsed-time difference
comes from calls, stack traffic, allocation placement or pointer dependencies.
